A sensible orderCompletion roadmapFive phases

ESO is too large for a single linear checklist. Treat these phases as lanes that overlap: begin the slow account-wide grinds early, then finish demanding group and PvP goals when your character is ready.

01

Build the foundation

Reach level 50, work through the main quest and guild stories, and begin trait research, fishing, motifs and skyshards immediately.

02

Clear the world

Finish alliance and chapter stories zone by zone. Pair quests with delves, world bosses, collectibles and public dungeons.

03

Learn group content

Complete normal dungeon “Scout” achievements first, then return for veteran clears and mechanic-specific challenges.

04

Take on endgame

Prepare builds and groups for trials, veteran arenas and the harder dungeon achievements. Use the part trackers for boss lists.

05

Schedule PvP and mop-up

Plan Emperor with an organised campaign group. Finish Cyrodiil, Imperial City, Battlegrounds and any remaining long-term collections.

Start early: Jewelry research, Master Fisher, motifs and skyshards are poor last-minute grinds.

Account-wide caveat: Most in-game achievements are account-wide, but motif knowledge, skyshards, lorebooks and some museum or public-dungeon progress can remain character-specific.

How progress and part tracking work

Achievement checks and optional counters are stored only in this browser. Part tracking is a planning aid; the in-game achievement panel remains authoritative.

The full catalogue is maintained in achievements.json, so future chapters can be added without rebuilding the interface.

Achievement names and requirements are based on the supplied list and checked against TrueAchievements. Account-wide behavior and in-game tracking guidance come from ESO Support. The Elder Scrolls Online is owned by ZeniMax Online Studios and Bethesda Softworks.
