01 · First community

Getting started

New to the game? Play the tutorial first.

Start the tutorial on Green difficulty and play for a couple of hours. It introduces the mechanics at a comfortable pace and makes every later run easier. The tutorial normally gives you preset survivors and starts on Providence Ridge.

Once you understand the basics, leave that practice community behind and start your own run with the tutorial skipped. That lets you choose your survivors and map immediately.

◆

Recommended home map

Use Trumbull Valley on Green for your long-term mule community: play solo, level survivors, clear miscellaneous achievements and stockpile useful gear.

↗

Why Trumbull Valley?

The Mickey Wilkerson storyline appears naturally as you play and unlocks achievements along the way. Complete those quests whenever they appear.

What completes a campaign run?

  1. Follow the opening story objectives and promote a leader.
  2. Destroy every Plague Heart on the map.
  3. Finish that leader’s legacy questline.

Most routine story objectives are low risk—usually conversations, scavenging or building at base. Plague Heart clearing is the time-consuming and dangerous part.

04 · The dangerous bit

Clearing Plague Hearts

Find them

  1. Look for locations outlined in red on the map.
  2. Find a nearby binoculars icon.
  3. Drive there, climb the survey point and scout the surrounding buildings.

Destroy them

  1. Park where you can see into the infected building.
  2. Climb onto the vehicle roof to stay out of reach.
  3. Use a .50-cal weapon on the Heart when you have a clear angle.
  4. For enclosed spawns, go inside prepared with explosives or mines.
Plan for a long session: Lethal maps can contain up to 28 Plague Hearts. A full clear can take roughly 4–8 hours depending on spawns and how smoothly the run goes.
05 · Staying alive and progressing

Useful habits

Play with others

Joining other communities is a strong way to earn influence and resources. It makes later building and upgrades much easier.

Gain standing quickly

Let anyone collect supply rucksacks, but swap to the survivor you want to promote before depositing them. The standing gain goes to the character who turns them in.

Maximum morale

This should come naturally on a Dread run. Cook hot meals, manage facilities, meet survivor needs and keep infestations under control.

Bounties and landmarks

Take Bounty Broker challenges as you play, and claim Landmark Outposts across the route for their related achievements.

A semi-cheese for avoiding death

If a character is close to dying, quit and load them into a second player’s Green-difficulty game. A short Blood Plague timer can become dramatically longer, giving you time to cure the infection, heal, reload and prepare before returning to the harder community.

Danger on Lethal→Quit safely→Join Green game→Heal and resupply→Return prepared

This requires another player or a safe session to join. Your mule community is still valuable as storage and preparation space, but moving a survivor between your own communities is not a normal instant-swap mechanic.

06 · Remaining modes

Daybreak and Heartland

Horde mode

Daybreak

Expect to fail often at first. Treat the mode as a long-term project and chip away at its achievements and unlocks between campaign sessions.

Story mode

Heartland

Plan for two full playthroughs. Choose one character set and decision path on the first run, then take the opposite options on the second. This covers the missable branches efficiently.

Important: base-game survivors cannot be brought into Heartland.

This is a practical community route, not a requirement to play perfectly. Difficulty, balance and reward details can change with game updates.
